      How I work

      • I think like an owner: I look after the whole outcome, not just my task.
      • I start by understanding how people actually do their job, then fix or build around that, not the other way round.
      • I like things simple. If a screen needs explaining, I go back and fix the screen.
      • I stay involved after launch, because the real feedback starts once people use it.
      • I would rather say “I don’t know yet” and find out than guess.
